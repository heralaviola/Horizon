### [Four AI Giants Sued Over Alleged Coordination to Slow AI Development](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/09/18/anthropic-openai-spacexai-google-sued-over-calls-to-pace-ai-development-01085023) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Anthropic, OpenAI, SpaceXAI, and Google have been sued in a California federal court on antitrust claims that their public statements calling for a slowdown in AI development amounted to an illegal coordinated effort. The complaint, filed by consumers subscribed to the companies&\#x27; AI services, alleges that Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei&\#x27;s recent public call for the industry to jointly slow advanced AI development was echoed by public endorsements from Elon Musk, Sam Altman, and Demis Hassabis, forming an unlawful restraint of trade under Section 1 of the Sherman Antitrust Act. **「Background」** Section 1 of the Sherman Antitrust Act prohibits contracts, combinations, or conspiracies that unreasonably restrain trade, and has historically been applied to agreements among competitors that limit output or coordinate pricing. The lawsuit follows a broader public debate in 2023 and 2024 in which AI executives, including Musk and Altman, called for temporary pauses or slowdowns in AI development, citing safety risks from rapidly advancing capabilities.

**「Impact」** If the case proceeds and the court finds the public statements constituted an illegal conspiracy, it could establish a precedent that public advocacy by competing AI firms on development timelines may carry antitrust liability, potentially chilling future industry-wide safety discussions or coordinated governance efforts.

### [Xcode 27.1 Hides iPhone Duo Simulator Action Bar](https://x.com/itspdfu/status/2101038602528375181) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Xcode 27.1 includes a hidden action bar for the iPhone Duo simulator that enables device angle adjustment, posture switching, and desktop mode. The feature is activated by running the defaults command \`defaults write com.apple.dt.Devices com.apple.dt.coredevicepop.useInternalV68ActionBar -bool true\` and restarting Device Hub. The action bar provides keyboard shortcuts for five device postures and a desktop mode toggle, and the iPhone Duo simulator also includes a hidden HingeStatePoster wallpaper that displays the fold angle.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 19, 10:40

**「Simulator Tooling for Foldable Devices」** Apple&\#x27;s iOS Simulator has historically provided limited tooling for non-standard device form factors, with most developer-facing controls exposed through the Device Hub interface. The iPhone Duo simulator represents a newer category of foldable device simulation that requires additional posture and hinge state controls not present in standard iPhone simulators.

**「Developer Access to Foldable Simulation Controls」** iOS developers building apps for foldable devices can now access advanced simulation controls for testing posture-dependent layouts and hinge-aware interactions, though the feature remains hidden and requires manual activation via the defaults command.
