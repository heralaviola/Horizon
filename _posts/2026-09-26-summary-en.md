### [NumPy MLP with GUI for Live Training Visualization](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1wqy1qd/p_a_small_mlp_from_scratch_in_numpy_with_a_gui_to/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A from-scratch NumPy implementation of a small multilayer perceptron with manual backpropagation, SGD with momentum, L2 regularization, dropout, cosine decay, and four activation functions, achieving 98.5% accuracy on the full MNIST training set. The accompanying GUI visualizes training dynamics in real time, including per-mini-batch and per-epoch loss, per-layer gradient norms, inactive neuron percentages, weight distributions compared to initialization, and first-layer receptive fields. Additional features include PCA and t-SNE visualizations of the test set per layer, robustness curves for noise and rotation, confidence threshold coverage versus accuracy, and an interactive lab for neuron ablation, pruning, weight noise injection, and softmax temperature adjustment with immediate test accuracy feedback. The project is open source on GitHub and targets ML educators, self-learners, and instructors.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/No-Brain-1655 · Sep 26, 18:38

**「Manual Backpropagation in NumPy」** Implementing neural networks without autograd frameworks requires manually deriving and coding the forward and backward passes, a common educational exercise to teach how gradients flow through layers. This project extends that approach by adding a graphical interface to make the typically opaque training process visible and interactive.

**「Pedagogical Tool for Neural Network Transparency」** The tool provides ML educators and learners with a transparent, interactive way to observe how neural networks behave during training, including how individual neurons contribute to predictions through ablation and pruning experiments. Its from-scratch implementation and real-time visualizations may help students understand concepts like gradient flow, overfitting, and model robustness more intuitively than black-box frameworks.

rss · Sean Goedecke · Sep 26, 00:00

**「Background」** The software engineering landscape has shifted dramatically from the ZIRP era, when abundant investment and engineer-friendly policies created a unique environment of job security and influence. Today&\#x27;s newcomers face a different reality shaped by economic tightening and the rise of AI tools, making much traditional advice—often rooted in that earlier context—potentially misleading or even harmful for junior engineers who lack the leverage to safely follow it.

**「Solution」** Sean Goedecke advises beginning engineers to prioritize pragmatism, conscientiousness, and independent judgment over political activism or resistance to change. He emphasizes forming your own opinions and maintaining confidence in your relevance, as the nature of the job may change but being smart, friendly, and conscientious remains valuable.

**「Takeaway」** The central thesis is that beginning software engineers should adapt to the current era&\#x27;s realities by focusing on being genuinely useful, thinking independently about AI, and avoiding political risks—recognizing that advice shaped by the ZIRP era&\#x27;s conditions no longer applies. Success now depends less on making grand gestures and more on consistent helpfulness, deep understanding, and maintaining one&\#x27;s own judgment in the face of rapid technological change.

### [10-Year Treasury Yield Hits 19-Year High of 5.23%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/26/10-year-treasury-yield-is-at-its-highest-in-19-years-how-we-got-here.html) ⭐️ 9.0/10

The 10-year Treasury yield reached 5.23% on Friday, its highest level since 2007, up from just below 4.8% earlier this month, driven by sticky inflation, a 64% market expectation of a Federal Reserve rate hike in October, and heavy bond issuance from government deficits and AI infrastructure spending.

rss · CNBC Finance · Sep 26, 13:30

**「Background」** Bond yields rise when bond supply increases or when investors demand higher returns to compensate for inflation and rate-risk; the combination of large government deficits and roughly $132 billion in corporate debt issued by major tech companies through July has expanded bond supply, pressuring yields upward.

**「Impact」** Higher Treasury yields increase borrowing costs for households \(mortgages\), corporations \(business loans\), and governments, while making bonds more attractive relative to stocks, thereby affecting investment decisions across financial markets.
